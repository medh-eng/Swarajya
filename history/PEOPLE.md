# People (dramatis personae) and spelling sheet

One spelling per name, used everywhere (cards, briefings, History notes). Marathi forms in
Devanagari go into the localisation files later; the English spelling below is the master.
Every person shown on a card must have a row here and at least one chronology ref.

Basis column: what we may show about them. `TRADITION` people (or deeds) are labelled in game.
