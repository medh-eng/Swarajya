# Contested points and how the game handles them

Shivaji Maharaj is revered across Maharashtra and India, and several points of his story are
argued over by historians and, sometimes, in politics. The game's rule: **show the evidence, name
the disagreement, pick one version for play, and say which one we picked.** Never resolve a real
dispute by inventing certainty.

Numbers below are referenced from `CHRONOLOGY.md`.

| # | Question | The versions | Game's handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Birth date** | 19 Feb 1630 (Julian; = 1 Mar 1630 Gregorian), from the Jedhe-family chronology, adopted by the Government of Maharashtra. Older tradition: April 1627 (6 or 10 April). Many also celebrate by the Hindu calendar (Phalgun Vadya Tritiya). | Show "Shivneri, 1630". In the History note: the tithi, the 19 February date, and one line on the 1627 tradition. |
| 2 | **Torna: 1645, 1646 or 1647?** | Most references 1646 (age 16). Some popular sources 1645 or 1647. | Use 1646. |
| 3 | **Fateh Khan at Purandar, 1648** | Popular retellings describe a battle; not yet found in a primary source by us. | `VERIFY`. No chapter until found in Sabhasad, Shivabharata or Jedhe. |
| 4 | **How Jaoli was taken (1656)** | Maratha accounts include a pretended marriage embassy and a surprise; Chandrarao More was later executed after trying to escape. Modern historians call the circumstances controversial. | **Purandare (pp. 207-212) has no marriage embassy at all** (a quarrel over Gunjan Mawal, warnings, then a campaign with the Deshmukhs); the pretended embassy stays only as one disputed popular account. Chapter `ch03_jaoli` is played as a straight assault. The History note says plainly that the method is disputed and gives both accounts. No card celebrates the deception. |
| 5 | **Pratapgad: who struck first?** | Maratha sources (Sabhasad, Shivabharata, the ballads): Afzal Khan seized Shivaji and struck first; Shivaji was armed for that. Sources hostile to Shivaji (Persian/Bijapuri): Shivaji killed him at a parley. The English letter of Dec 1659 says the queen told Afzal to pretend friendship. Army sizes in the bakhars are inflated. | The Bhet (parley) minigame lets either side prepare for treachery. The History note gives the Maratha account, the Bijapuri account and the Revington letter, and says certainty is not possible. No troop numbers. |
| 6 | **Dadoji Kondadeo and Samarth Ramdas as "gurus"** | Dadoji administered Shahaji's Pune jagir (documented). Ramdas's role as guru is also disputed by many historians. | **Pon's decision (D-15, 24 Nov 2026): Dadoji Kondadeo is shown as Shivaji's teacher**, following *Raja Shivachhatrapati*. His card and the History notes present this as Purandare's account, and the History panel says in one line that historians dispute it. Samarth Ramdas: no change (does not appear). |

| 10 | **Date of the Agra escape** | 17 Aug 1666 (Britannica, most references); one article gives 22 July. | 17 August 1666. |
| 11 | **"Sinhagad" named for Tanaji?** | Popular: Shivaji renamed Kondhana "Sinhagad" (lion's fort) after Tanaji fell.
