## Why he's not just "Bijapur" like Rustam Zaman and Fazl Khan

Siddi Hilal served under Afzal Khan at Pratapgad, then switched to Shivaji's side and fought for
Swarajya from the Battle of Kolhapur onward — confirmed by two independent sources (Wikipedia's "Battle
of Pratapgad" article plus the existing Kolhapur/Panhala source), resolving `history/CONTESTED.md` #29.
He's already in the game: Chapter 5 (Kolhapur) decks him on Shivaji's side (`basis: HISTORY`), and the
chapter is built and playable — it's just drawing a placeholder frame in place of his art right now.

## Format

5:7 portrait, 1500 × 2100 minimum. Save to `art/source/cards/sw_siddi_hilal.png`, then run
`npm run assets`.

## STYLE block (same as batch 1 and 3 — paste before the prompt)

> Painting in the manner of 17th-century Deccan and Maratha court miniatures (Bijapur school, Maratha
> court portraits): opaque watercolour and gouache on aged paper, fine brush outlines, rich flat colour
> fields with subtle shading, gold-leaf highlights, delicate patterned textiles; blended with a warm
> "living miniature" atmosphere (soft lamplight or low sun, depth, a little mist). **Swarajya palette**:
> Sahyadri basalt grey, monsoon-cloud blue-grey, red laterite earth, saffron, deep teal and cream — never
> green (D-21: green belongs to Bijapur, not Shivaji's side). Dignified, historically careful, painterly,
> no caricature, no gore. Paint to the edge of the picture (no deckled paper edge). Portrait 5:7: clear
> sky above the head, head top about a quarter of the way down, 3/4-length figure, lower quarter simple.

## AVOID block (same as every batch — paste after the prompt)

> No text, letters, numerals, logos, watermarks, borders, frames or signatures. No modern clothing, no
> film or television likeness of any real person, no statue-like or cartoon faces, no blood or gore, no
> religious symbols used as a banner of one faith against another, no modern objects.

## `sw_siddi_hilal` — Siddi Hilal · epic · *Cavalry Commander*

*Basis: HISTORY [C-1659-PRATAPGAD, C-1659-KOLHAPUR, C-1660-PANHALA-SIEGE, C-1672-SALHER]. Vega, Deploy —
your other units in this line get +1 attack: a rallying officer, the Swarajya mirror of Rustam Zaman's
own ability.*

**Prompt:** A composed, battle-hardened Abyssinian cavalry commander in Swarajya service: close-cropped
beard, a plain saffron-and-cream turban (no court jewels — a working officer, not a courtier), mounted on
a dark horse with a curved sword drawn, Maratha lancers visible behind him in cold dawn light over open
winter grassland. Steady and sure of himself — a man who has already chosen his side and isn't looking
back. Dignified, no reference to his earlier service with Afzal Khan anywhere in the image.
